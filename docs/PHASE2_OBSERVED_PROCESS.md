# Phase 2 taxonomy-to-process record

## Outcome

Phase 2 closes the taxonomy-to-observed-process boundary for the manuscript's
tail and MSE results.  The original 41 supplied modules remain byte-for-byte
unchanged.  Two additive modules define the realised process and expose
class-facing theorem interfaces.

## Realised observed process

`RealizedObservedProcess` bundles:

- one realised revelation order at each latent state;
- the derived stage index `min n (K.horizon omega)`, including constant
  extension after termination;
- a filtration and adaptedness of every realised stage value;
- strong measurability and `L²` moment assumptions;
- `ClassEventsMeasurable` for the three global taxonomy events; and
- measurability of every realised persistence event.

The value process is derived from the hypothetical estimand and the realised
order.  It is not an independently supplied sequence, so it cannot drift from
the structural object used by `HasClass`.

corresponding `HasClass` proof, rather than a second proof of monotonicity,
fixedness, or absorbing coverage.

## Representation boundary

The abstract structural model does not give `Prefix` a measurable space, so
the process records filtration-adaptedness of its realised values rather than
claiming that the filtration is definitionally equal to the sigma-algebra
generated by raw prefixes.  The concrete observation models planned for
Phase 3 can instantiate this field with their generated filtrations.
